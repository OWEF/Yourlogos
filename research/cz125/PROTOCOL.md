## A — Learning objectives, exact constructs

**Dillard / Token Studio.** Ordinary uses of days can accumulate into patterns; allocating six half-hour slots over five imagined repeated evenings **does not** reveal actual habits, motives, personal worth, or morally rank writing above rest or relationships. Test both cumulative logic and epistemic limits.

**Kierkegaard / Swipe Decision.** Action precedes full knowledge; a retrospective interpretation cannot provide actual foresight, a fully outside-time standpoint or the unchosen future. Agency sequence must remain **USER_DECISION → AUTHORED_DEVELOPMENT (fictional event, not reader control) → USER_DECISION → RETROSPECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**. Test both user actions versus authored event and uncertainty about the alternative.

## C — Fixed per-game procedure (approx. 8–12 minutes)

The two games use the **same** stages in the specified order, not the same game repeated twice.

**C0. Before source quote/content:** Present only a *neutral plain dilemma*, independently reviewable prospective texts:
- Dillard: **"You have three free hours this evening. You could write, answer messages, or rest."**
- Kierkegaard: **"You have an offer to take a new job. You can accept it or remain in your current job."**

These two exact dilemma sentences are **prospective operational clarification N1** (not original author text), **approved by founder on 16 November 2026 before any enrollment**, and are deliberately declarative to avoid demanding a choice before baseline. Independent exact-SHA methodological verification remains required before recruitment. Do **not** display the actual quote, explanatory answer, source name, moral inference or unseen-counterfactual distinction with these baseline prompts. Ask original question **"What is the dilemma? Which parts could you decide yourself?"** Capture verbatim and an independent *baseline philosophical comprehension* rating 0–4 after the answer is recorded (not participant auto-score).

**C1. On first intro screen, before user action:** Ask original exact question **"In your own words, what is this experience asking you to do? What will it help you see?"** Capture verbatim and whether coaching was needed. Because this must happen *before play*, keep recorder open in separate tab and save a fresh private local export after first-glance before resuming interaction.

**C2. Play independently:** Observe actual tap/swipe/keyboard interaction, ability to edit/back, mistakes, whether they treat the authored hypothetical event as a chosen action, and all intervention/coaching language. Capture exact canonical Dillard amounts/repetitions or Kierkegaard three-stage path from the accepted prototype (never guess it). Keep quote/three-section wording unchanged. Log technical failure as failure, not made-up human success.

**C3. After all three sections, before feedback:** Prompt original exact: **"What is the philosopher's main point? How did your decisions or events illustrate it? Which parts were not within your control or not known?"** Capture verbatim. Independent human scorer gives **0–4 post** after capture. Neither form nor code supplies correct answers.

**C4. Misconception discrimination, with *reason* rejecting incorrect alternatives:** Display exactly:

**Dillard:**  
A "Five imagined evenings can illustrate a time-use pattern, but don't prove real-life priorities."  
B "More writing tokens prove writing is the better way to live."  
C "The game can tell exactly what you do on an average night."  
Researcher ONLY key: **A**; 2/2 requires correct A **and justified rejection of B and C**, not mere guessing.

**Kierkegaard:**  
A "Later invented events show the unchosen job would have been worse."  
B "You chose your response, but not which real event happened; the unchosen path remains unknown."  
C "Looking backward lets you judge the first decision with complete certainty."  
Researcher ONLY key: **B**; 2/2 requires correct B **and justified rejection of A and C**.

Do not display answer keys in participant-facing HTML/JS or before responses. Participant-facing instruction ("Choose one statement and explain why; explain why the other two are misleading") is **administrative clarification**, not a new source question.

**C5. Unseen transfer, separately recorded:**  
Dillard: **"A reader models a week with two hours of reading and five hours of rest each day. What can they conclude, and what can't they?"**  
Kierkegaard: **"A person moves city and discovers later that the apartment is noisier than advertised. Does this prove moving was foolish or that staying would have been better? Why?"**

Transfer is **separate qualitative evidence**. The original Section E defines no numerical transfer dimension or separate threshold, so no fabricated transfer score or pass criterion.

**C6. Qualitative engagement, original exact prompt:** **"Which moment was clearest? What confused you? What if anything was worth remembering? Which interaction felt more meaningful and why?"** Capture neutral verbatim observations, including boring, confusing and benefit statements without numeric engagement score or inference of learning.

Complete stages C0–C6 for the **other** source/game using the same protocol with the Section B counterbalance.

## F — Original fixed SUCCESS thresholds — do NOT amend

Exploratory formative **ACCEPT recommendation requires every condition**:

1. Interaction: **>=4/5 people score 2/2 in EACH game**, and no unaddressed recurring event-versus-agency confusion.
2. Philosophy: **>=4/5 people score >=3/4 AFTER EACH game**.
3. Measured gain: **>=3/5 people improve >=1 point baseline→post in at least one game, without regression in the other**.
4. Misconceptions: **>=4/5 people score 2/2 counterexample understanding in EACH game**.
5. Follow-up compliance: valid **20–28h** follow-up from **>=4/5** distinct people.
6. Delayed BOTH: **>=3/5 total people achieve 2/2 recall for BOTH ideas**.
7. CZ-121 signs off **BOTH** source passages and interpretive bridges without unresolved material error, CZ-123 identifies **NO unresolved P0 philosophical mistake**. Physical CZ-126 is a **separate mandatory mobile-release** gate.
8. Engagement quotes and observed confusion reported without numerical cutoff; fun does NOT override missing learning.
9. Single material false source attribution, invented reader agency or fabricated future is a **BLOCK** even if other measures favorable.
10. Recurrent confusion or unmet thresholds → **REVISE**. Missing qualified source review, no genuine participants or inadequate delayed compliance → **NOT YET EVALUABLE** (do not call it a fail for fabricated absence). Five-person formative results **cannot** establish population-level causal effectiveness.

Denominators are **five unique participant IDs**; per-game observations exactly five in each game (ten rows total); no count substitution using ten "people." All exclusions/missing/assisted responses remain in evidence, not silently discarded.
