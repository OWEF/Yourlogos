# YourLogos M2 R4-A — Narrative & Philosophical Failure Matrix

**Parent R3:** `67605e5d9ccc1dea03d56244c0cf51cf5ed93630` · **Engineering:** PR #248 (stacked draft) · **Source:** inherited ID47, George Long, *Enchiridion* §1. Editorially provisional; no new admission.

## Diagnosis, with the action recap hidden

The R3 observation was **only** `Message delivered. Later, no reply has arrived.` The shared question — “What am I asking the other person's reply to prove about whether I have done my part?” — presupposed the participant wanted an answer as moral certification. Six distinct recorded action histories collapsed into four semantic classes: acknowledgement+invitation, private preparation, second apology, and withheld acknowledgement. Convergence within equivalent classes was valid; the flaw was that **nothing new happened that made the differences demand reflection**.

## Philosophical attribution boundary

- **Source-supported:** Epictetus differentiates what is within one's own judgment/acts from what belongs to others (§1). He does not grant omnipotence over opportunities, effectiveness, other agents, or downstream consequences.
- **Fiction-supported:** The participant sent the chosen message. A distinct group notice announces a meeting about the same idea; it was not caused by the sent message. Later actions may be planned or promised but have not happened.
- **Interpretive, not stipulated by Epictetus:** Whether a second apology, public acknowledgement or private invitation is ethically preferable; what further responsibility is actually owed. A participant may reject Stoic categories and still consider these tensions.
- **Counterfactual only:** A friendly reply would not erase the prior public interruption; no reply ever arriving does not eliminate future possibilities. Neither response is part of the canonical event ledger.

## Predetermined-lesson guard

The experiment is **not** a test of whether a participant apologises enough. Sending another message, privately planning a public correction, and preparing to listen protect different values and carry different opportunity costs. The final reasoning uses the same shared Reasoning → Emotion Strategy → Expression pipeline as the other source-authored pilots, with four fact-predicate interpretations rather than six bespoke speeches. Equivalent public promises and equivalent private preparations intentionally converge after removing action recaps.

## Failure conditions and unresolved work

Any claim that the participant *already* performed their announced public behaviour, that the friend read the message, that the group event was caused by the first message, or that Epictetus prescribed a specific etiquette is a release blocker. Separate human review must still test whether the narrative is engaging when the ending is hidden and whether an uncoached participant can transfer the argument to an unfamiliar scenario.

**Stage gate:** Automated narrative tests and semantic traces are engineering evidence only. No human-learning PASS, no sealed CZ-154 QA, no source publication, merge, staging or production action.
