# Expression Engine 1.0 · founder gate

Built above the three-stage Discover draft #161 and its reasoning dependency #160. The prototype is deterministic, runs offline, and has zero model calls or per-reading inference cost. No account state or production route is accessed by the prototype.

## Information flow

1. The existing mechanism validates the allocation or the ordered decisions. The consequence packet identifies one conceptual distinction and preserves its warrant, source, uncertainty, route, visual and contextual question. The interrupted evening with no writing now compares a fully assigned evening with an intention that has no sitting; neither rest nor replies can be established as the cause.
2. Meaning selection reads that one fired distinction, its evidence and its source references. Strategy selection uses a small editorial map: repeated deferral and deciding twice admit surprise; a fragile boundary can warrant challenge; a repeatable day with rest and writing can invite play. Other rules default to reflection. Unknown distinctions retain their base reading. Overrides are confined to the research interface.
3. Composition expresses the validated distinction with a reviewed headline and builds the observation from the actual budget and influence or the selected route. The same source passage, distinction, limits and contextual question apply across voices. The existing shell renders one recommended voice. Relevant My World material stays inside the first panel. The last ordinary panel has exactly one question.
4. The founder HTML is generated from the identical application and module sources. It adds a comparison below the ordinary result with all four readings and a collapsed meaning/evidence panel. Use Back to choices to vary a single decision. The normal build omits this comparison.

## Philosophical QA

| Path | Actual recommended headline | Editorial assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Dillard · no writing, one hour replies, two hours rest, interruptions | Every hour has a place. The story still has none. | Accurate because all three uses are explicit. The surprise is that a full accounting of time still leaves a standing intention unprovided for. Repetition links the case to Dillard. The phrasing gives the story emotional weight without assigning guilt or ranking rest. |
| Kierkegaard · stay, team changes, continue | You stayed twice. | The first decision concerned a familiar job; the second happened after a change. What sounds like one continuous choice becomes two decisions made with different evidence. The headline is natural and short, but needs the next sentence to make sense. |
| Dillard · all writing, deliberately planned | The story has booked the whole evening. | The reservation metaphor suits this low-stakes setting. The philosophical question is what further arrangements would make such a day repeatable. |
| Kierkegaard · move, miss friends, adapt | Missing your friends gives you a new reason to act. | The default stays reflective. A playful rendering of distance can feel too light; it is available to compare in the lab. The outcome of a new plan remains unknown. |

**Assessment across voices.** Factual accuracy and source fidelity are the strongest properties: the allocation is fixed, the imagined event is attributed to its route, and the source explanation remains stable. Novelty is strongest for the twice-made choice. Emotional resonance is an editorial hypothesis; keeping feelings tied to the imagined branch avoids inferring distress. Reflective language reads most naturally for the friendship case. The playful language works better for a calendar than for separation. The four versions keep the main reading short; repeating exact time amounts can still sound like bookkeeping. Questions remain specific to repetition, prospective action or the limits of hindsight; tone alone does not change the inquiry.

**Engineering evidence and limits.** Tests enumerate all 84 valid evenings and eight routes across four voices, plus the founder regression. Browser QA exercises the three stages, independent Like/Discover actions, editable paths, optional labeled history, keyboard focus, reduced motion and the comparison view. This proves the contract and interactions. Founder acceptance, comprehension with new readers, and physical iPhone Safari require separate review. New rules can render reflectively before richer diction is curated. The side-by-side prototype is a public research link, not an authenticated admin system; it contains no private account data.
