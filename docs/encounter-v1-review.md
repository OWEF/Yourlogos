# Discovery quality matrix · Encounter 1.1

Founder review of the existing two-Encounter pilot. The following observations, consequences, warrants, discoveries and limits are taken from the evaluated packets. Editorial judgments are separate. The preview presents the discovery first and permits a question-free ending. The optional prompt occupies no space when absent.

## How the inference works

The existing mechanism validates choices. The consequence model derives a relation from those facts and the applicable source interpretation. Each relation records its two concepts, supporting premises, warrant, and open question of fact. The packet binds source references before the expression function projects display fields. For the interrupted-majority example the model computes two feasible responses to an additional half-hour reply. When there is no rest time left, maintaining writing would instead require deferring a reply. These are counterfactual probes; the discovery leaves the actual booking order unknown. The route model keeps what was available before the choice, later experience, retrospective interpretation, and next action separately inspectable.

## Annie Dillard

The schedule passage relates daily practice to a life through repetition. Time allocations are scenario evidence. No duration proves how highly someone values the activity. [Reproduced passage](https://www.saltproject.org/progressive-christian-blog/2023/4/24/how-we-spend-our-days-by-annie-dillard)

## Søren Kierkegaard

The short form condenses JJ:167 (1843). Reflection occurs while living continues. The job story illustrates that limit; the journal offers no decision procedure for employment. [Danish and attributed English translation](https://homepage.divms.uiowa.edu/~jorgen/kierkegaardquotesource.html)

### evidence for a next step / evidence of an earlier error

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|

| Observation | You take the new job → You miss your old friends → You make a new plan |
| Consequence | You miss your old friends. You make a new plan. |
| Hidden distinction | The concrete experience of distance first enters after moving; the new plan acts on it. Its later availability cannot establish a neglected fact at the deadline. |
| Discovery without prompt | Missing your friends gives you a reason to change how you keep in touch. That reason became clear through moving. You can take it seriously now while leaving open whether moving was a mistake. |

| Editorial judgment | Promising. A present reason to care for friends can arise without a verdict on the original move. |

## Editorial assessment

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Grounding | Strongest when a single later fact changes what action is possible. Dillard’s planning answer applies to the whole evening; it cannot identify a protected block. |
| Non-obviousness | The further-message probe and the cost of waiting each expose a consequence the participant never selected. The joint-rest-and-writing ending is weaker because coexistence is already visible in the sliders. |
| Path sensitivity | Changing the motive at identical hours changes which explanation is available. Changing the response to missing friends distinguishes a new reason to act from a forecast. Equivalent allocations and routes within a rule share wording because exact durations cannot identify a different scheduling policy. |
| Comprehensibility | Endings use ordinary examples. The protected-time ending asks the reader to imagine one extra message. Its clarity needs a first-time reader who has not seen the matrix. |
| Philosophical value | For Dillard the issue is what can recur; for Kierkegaard it is what looking backward can settle while another decision is already due. Attributing a moral duty to contact friends would go beyond the selected path. The optional question leaves that assessment open. |

No live history is read. A single decision supplies no evidence of a stable trait. The strict majority threshold is a conservative editorial gate for the prominent-replies example; it marks no universal boundary of intentional action. Neither the number of passing tests nor this desk review establishes felt discovery for a first-time reader.
