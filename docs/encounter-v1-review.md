## Architecture and scope

The v0.2 case selects a source, a philosophical operation, an everyday scenario and a fitted mechanism. The allocator supplies observed hours and a conditional repetition. The branching validator supplies an ordered, compatible route. The consequence module takes those validated facts and interprets them through two different rulesets. Its sidecar packet keeps input, observation, tension, consequence, discovery, optional question, evidence, rule trace, sources and limits separately inspectable. Personal relevance is expressed through the path-specific discovery. The expression function projects four fields without generating claims. Invalid or unsupported inputs yield a declined packet.

The original Insight Packet validator permits only quotation IDs 49 and 28. This pilot versions its own companion contract for IDs 12 and 34. fingerprintEvidence is null, reserved for a separately reviewed exploration event compatible with source/quoteId/theme. The page keeps only in-memory interaction state.

## Source boundaries

Dillard's schedule passage describes the structure that allows work to continue across days. The three activities and five-evening window are editorial choices. The wording and surrounding paragraph were corroborated in reproduced excerpts. Dillard remains outside the 15-record Atlas. Check an authorized print edition before approving the source for publication. [Excerpt](https://www.saltproject.org/progressive-christian-blog/2023/4/24/how-we-spend-our-days-by-annie-dillard)

Kierkegaard's 1843 JJ:167, SKS 18, p. 306, continues past the familiar short form. Reflection takes place while life continues, so the backward view itself remains incomplete. The source page gives the Danish and an identified English translation. The offer and all eight possible paths are invented illustrations. [Text and translation note](https://homepage.divms.uiowa.edu/~jorgen/kierkegaardquotesource.html)

## Open review gates

The single influence answer cannot identify which particular message or obligation mattered. General readings deliberately leave that unspecified. The week projection describes opportunity to write, never completion of the story. The second fork in the map is a user-selected imagined event, so judgments about personal feelings and forecasts would be unsupported. Some readers may still hear the stay/team example primarily as practical career advice. Check whether the next decision makes the journal's temporal question legible to them.

All seven Dillard rule families and eight Kierkegaard routes are reachable. Software tests check sensitivity, rejection, stale conclusions and evidence. Claims about felt discovery and clarity still need first-time readers. Ask a reader what they learned before opening the QA drawer, then change one choice and ask whether the new ending still fits.
