# Semantic reasoning transfer: architecture increment above #163

The existing 1.1 constraint solver and transition evaluator remain the structural foundation. A new semantic pipeline constructs scenarios from sourced context and model data, validates tensions, derives classified claims, ranks a meaning and passes it to the existing expression interface. No quote ID is used in the new reasoning or composition module.

## General operations

- Compare explicit stated allocation targets with actual amounts, without inferring motivation.
- Calculate repeated resource demands under a declared constant-allocation horizon, without predicting habit formation.
- Detect complementary output improvements and locally feasible opportunity costs.
- Distinguish information before acting from subsequent information; detect revisions and closed alternatives.
- Reject decision alternatives lacking commitments, duplicate commitments, or merely compatible assignments.
- Compose four modes from the same selected claim, source relation and question. The generated word count must be 45–70 or composition returns an editorial-review result. Conditional claims retain an assumption qualifier. Mode differences are intentionally modest rhetorical framing; they are not four fully developed literary voices yet.

## Transfer evidence

35 local tests pass, including all 31 prior tests. Development cases: money purchases, joint workshop production, interpersonal apology and disputed historical attribution. Additional post-implementation fixtures: archival staffing and disputed manuscript translation. These were evaluated without modifying the kernel. They are internal holdouts, not an independent blind evaluation. Existing Dillard/Kierkegaard regressions remain green but their old curated endings are not yet migrated to the new semantic contract.

## Honest limits / acceptance gaps

Source relation labels, concepts, assumptions, available actions and commitment assignments still require editorial judgment. The system does not verify philosophical truth from raw quotation text. The decision constructor shares a two-stage graph recipe; the underlying 1.1 evaluator supports longer graphs, but this new constructor is not an arbitrary scenario generator. Redundancy is checked structurally, not by unrestricted natural-language understanding. Counterfactuals remain local. Four modes preserve logic but vary only framing. The generic question depends on the selected operation and may need richer semantic variation. The engine does not yet choose between several equally warranted philosophical meanings through a validated philosophical salience model.

The new four examples reuse source records from #163; no new historical source claims were added. Epictetus and Descartes analogies still need independent philosophical review. The archival and manuscript fixtures demonstrate transfer within related structural domains, not universal philosophical coverage.
