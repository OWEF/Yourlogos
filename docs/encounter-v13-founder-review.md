## My World evidence

Pass the current user's existing buildWorldMemory(state, quotes) result to encounterConsequence as its optional third argument. The bridge examines only archived Living Thread note and opening fields. The existing archive records those fields from text volunteered by the reader. A bounded rule matches a complete inquiry or a first-person statement expressing the relevant relationship. The exact excerpt, record ID, source field, quote ID and available date are retained separately from the editorial connection. Application-generated questions, AI threads, broad themes, a save alone and ambiguous fragments produce no section. One path-specific closing question is shown, whether or not a history match exists.

The standalone preview operates without account data. Append #qa-reflection to demonstrate an explicitly labeled sample. Tests construct records in the application’s archive format and run the actual My World adapter. They do not access a person's private account. This first pass covers a limited range of English formulations, so relevant paraphrases can be missed. Supporting a wider vocabulary requires reviewing false positives as well as recall.

## Founder check

After a reader finishes, ask them to explain the quotation in their own words and name the choice that made its meaning clearer. Check the second decision in the friends route and the unobserved booking order in the writing route. Those are the two places where the interface could still lead a reader to claim more than the scenario establishes.
