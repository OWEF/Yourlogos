## Source fidelity

| Source | Primary meaning and legitimate transfer | Boundary / finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thoreau, *Walden*, “Economy” | The housing discussion measures acquisition against the portion of life spent obtaining it, questioning whether material improvements justify that sacrifice. A stated extra-work wage makes price-to-hours conversion an intelligible illustration. | The existing constant net wage and extra-shift assumptions are essential. Hours are not an exhaustive measure of life, welfare, or value. Zero purchases do not establish a better life; higher spending is not improved output. The truncated quotation should remain explicitly an excerpt: the original includes immediate and longer-term exchange. |
| Smith, *Wealth of Nations*, I.i | The pin example joins divided operations into one productive process. The printing/binding minimum illustrates why completed units depend on several contributions together. | Complementarity is a narrow analogy, not a demonstration of specialization's productivity advantage. Fixed invented rates cannot establish productivity relative to an unspecialized baseline, nor historical output, welfare, or an optimal social order. Keep this distinction visible in the result, not only metadata. |
| Epictetus, *Enchiridion*, §1 (with §4 qualification) | The text separates our judgments, pursuits and actions from things not ours, including reputation. Offering an apology can exemplify responsibility for one's own response while forgiveness remains the friend's. | Apologizing and subsequently giving requested space are compatible. A changed action-state does not logically retract a past action or prove hypocrisy. Physical success in sending is itself contingent; frame agency as choosing/attempting one's response, not omnipotence over external execution. Neither action establishes forgiveness, serenity, virtue, or indifference. |
| Descartes, *Discourse*, II | The first rule restrains assent beyond what is clear and distinct, avoiding haste and prejudice. An evidence check can illustrate restraint before assent. | Signed minutes are ordinary testimonial evidence, not Cartesian clear-and-distinct certainty. Existing limit is correct and indispensable. Withholding a claim does not refute it; new contrary evidence does not by itself prove an earlier judgment irrational given what was then known. |
| Dillard, *The Writing Life* excerpt | Hours accumulate into days and a life; a schedule makes a recurring arrangement possible. The repetition counterfactual legitimately changes the scale from one evening to a pattern. | Allocation does not reveal booking order, motive, quality, or future habit. Existing explicit hypotheticals and non-ranking of rest/replies are strengths. “Recovery” should not become an inferred physiological benefit merely because a slider is called rest. The web source reformats prose as verse; do not attribute that formatting to Dillard. |
| Kierkegaard, journal JJ:167 (1843) | Retrospective understanding and forward living are temporally asymmetric; the full entry denies a final stationary retrospective standpoint during temporal life. | The popular shortened quotation needs its excerpt/paraphrase status. Hypothetical job events can illustrate later understanding, never an observed prediction about this user. The unchosen life remains unobserved; a later cost cannot alone prove an original mistake. Existing records generally preserve these limits. |

## Incompatible or unsupported cases

1. **False agency mapping:** “Choose forgiveness” writes a friend's response as an effect of the user's apology. Decline that philosophical inference even if the graph is mechanically valid.
2. **Counterfeit Cartesian certainty:** a signed document automatically establishes indubitability. Decline the mapping; ordinary evidence can support a qualified report.
3. **Invalid conversion:** money comes from a gift or sunk savings, but the engine asserts additional work hours without an extra-work assumption. Decline the hours inference.
4. **False complementarity:** scanned, proofread and bound items are independently saleable outputs, yet a minimum is presented as their total. The declared production model does not fit.
5. **False historical contradiction:** an apology happened and later contact ceased. Do not decline merely because verbs or current-state values differ. Conversely, claiming the apology both happened and never happened in the same history is incompatible unless explicitly scoped as a retracted belief or corrected record.
6. **False source transfer:** Dillard's schedule is used to rank a person's worth, or Kierkegaard's retrospective understanding is used to guarantee hindsight's accuracy. Decline the interpretation rather than manufacturing source authority.

Verdict: the curated mappings are defensible limited teaching analogies. Their safe transfer depends on explicit domain assumptions and temporal scopes. Structural validity alone does not establish philosophical adequacy; human editorial approval and observed reader comprehension remain open.

## Follow-up review of implemented v18 behavior

Independently ran `node --test tests/semantic-v18-holdout.test.mjs`: all five tests passed. Read the actual semantic engine and fixtures and printed the four baseline readings. The apology reading now correctly retains both actions without calling them a revision. Descartes retains the crucial denial of Cartesian certainty. The new tests demonstrate arithmetic/graph generality and distinct histories. They do not by themselves verify all six philosophical incompatibility cases above.

**Assessment: curated improvement, not demonstrated general semantic adequacy.** `selection.central` is an author-supplied whitelist of claim kinds; the engine does not infer or test the fidelity of that mapping to a source. This can be a valid explicit editorial contract, provided it is described as such. It must not be represented as independent source interpretation. `selectionTrace` labels fidelity by whitelist membership, path relevance by nonempty evidence, and warrant by a classification string that the generator itself supplied. Those labels are records of decisions, not additional evidence for their correctness. Refusal when several central claims coexist is a useful conservative boundary.

Concrete findings from read-only probes:

1. **Unrelated revisions contaminate assent (implementation defect).** Add a second declared domain `contact={now,later}` with an opposition, then take `endorse → endorse` while changing contact from now to later. The engine says “You changed your stated position on the claim” even though `assent` remains `assent`. The assent claim uses global `changes.length`, not changes restricted to `model.assentField`. This fails the purpose of explicit semantic domains.
2. **Labels and declared semantics can conflict unnoticed.** Changing the first endorse option's commitment to `withhold` leaves its visible action “Endorse the attribution.” The route endorse → suspend then returns “You maintained your stated position on the claim.” The declared state controls reasoning while the contradictory visible action still appears in evidence. This is not safe acceptance of arbitrary authored scenarios.
3. **Agency compatibility remains trusted metadata.** Changing the first action to “Make your friend forgive you” still returns ready and composes that phrase with “forgiveness remains outside those choices.” The engine does not reject the false control premise. A textual caveat cannot resolve the contradictory premise. If free-text semantic checking is intentionally outside scope, require an explicit editorial validation status and keep unreviewed scenarios out of ready output.
4. **Missing source-specific assumptions remain outside validation.** Thoreau's selection points at every generated conversion claim regardless of whether the metric actually represents labor exchanged; Smith's selection points at every minimum-based dependency regardless of whether stages are indispensable. Mechanism compatibility is not enough to warrant either source mapping.
5. **Interpretive salience is still mostly prescribed.** All Smith paths select dependency and all Descartes paths select assent. The Smith baseline's evidence contains a useful imbalance `[6,1]` and feasible gain `1 → 2`, but the reading only reports one completed booklet and generic dependence. The Descartes reading names a change but does not explain the specific new evidence in the headline or experience. This is better than an irrelevant intention-gap selection, but remains thin educational transfer.
6. **Commitment relations need explicit scope.** Declared oppositions improve on comparing arbitrary state strings; however, all different undeclared pairs become `unresolved_relationship`, and assent can still be described as “maintained.” First-time declaration of a position is likewise not necessarily maintenance. Withholding assent is not denial of the proposition; any future truth-negation domain must distinguish those states.

The D1 fixture proves retained event history, not the full proposed cumulative event contract: it does not persist or assert `apologyOffered=true`. D2 usefully tests prerequisites and closure but its assertions do not establish that the prose communicates both publication history and correction. Add output-level semantic assertions and the cross-domain regression before claiming the v18 semantic gate is complete. No engine changes were made during this follow-up review.

### Executable scope versus editorial scope

The engine need not implement natural-language philosophy validation. Congruence among visible labels, structured commitments, source interpretation and source text is a **trusted curation boundary**, currently **not independently verified by the engine**. The conflicting-label and false-agency probes demonstrate that boundary; they are not a request for brittle keyword rules. Source-fidelity trace fields should make that provenance explicit rather than suggest the engine proved fidelity.

Useful executable contracts can stay structural: declare each modeled variable's owner (`self` or `external`) and kind (`event`, `commitment`, `observation`); allow chosen-action effects to write only self-owned variables; admit externally owned observations through separately identified developments; preserve occurred events monotonically so a later action cannot erase an apology or publication; scope revision claims to their own domains; require route-local evidence IDs for citation assertions. An agency claim then depends on an external variable that no own-action effect can assign. These checks enforce declared causal ownership and temporal scope without pretending to understand arbitrary prose or prove the philosophical interpretation.
