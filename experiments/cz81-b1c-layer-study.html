What is independently editable?

The SVG contains separately transformable head, eyes, eyelids and forelock, plus a body layer where the original head pixels are masked out to prevent a doubled face. The original portrait is embedded as a single local image resource, rather than redrawn as an unfamiliar mascot.

What still needs human review?

Occluded body/neck pixels have only provisional underpainting. The masks are hand-estimated and may leave seams or face/eye mismatches during movement. This is a hybrid raster/vector structure, not a fully traced vector illustration. We will not call it production-ready or artist-faithful until the founder compares it with CZ-23 on a real iPhone.
