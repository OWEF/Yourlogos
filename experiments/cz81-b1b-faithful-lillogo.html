CZ-81 B1b · STUDY
TECHNIQUE LIMITATION — READ FIRST

The original LilLogo art (little-logo-hd-v2.jpg, atlas little-logo-poses-v5.webp) is a flattened raster illustration. A true independent head, eye and eyelid rig cannot be reconstructed from it without redrawing or inventing anatomy, which this study refuses to do.

Instead, head and gaze changes come from the artist's own two painted poses (idle and curious) cross-faded at full opacity, plus whole-character micro-tilt of 1.8–3.8 degrees and a slow breathing scale. The tilt layer moves the entire sprite — it only reads as a head shift because the sprite is head-dominant. No blink is faked: a convincing eyelid would require drawing over the original eyes, so blinking is deliberately absent rather than fabricated.

WHAT THIS STUDY PROVES — AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

  • Two authored states (idle / curious) can be presented faithfully using only the original atlas cells, same-origin, offline.
  • Layered motion (tilt layer, breath layer, gaze-drift layer, pose layers) keeps amplitude subtle and independent in timing.
  • It does not prove an independent head rig, real blink, or verified CZ-23 identity in motion — those need a layered source file or approved redraw.
